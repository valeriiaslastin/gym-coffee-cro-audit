# Gym+Coffee — E-commerce CRO & Technical SEO Audit

An independent, self-directed UX heuristics, user journey friction, and technical SEO audit conducted for **Gym+Coffee** (`gympluscoffee.com`), a prominent Irish athleisure and lifestyle brand. 

* **Author:** Valeriia Slastin
* **Focus Area:** UX & Digital Growth Case Study
* **Date:** September 2026
* **Primary Deliverable:** Executive presentation deck (`GymCoffee_CRO_SEO_Audit_Valeriia_Slastin.pdf`)

---

## 🎯 About the Project
This independent case study evaluates the digital storefront of Gym+Coffee to identify critical leaks in the conversion funnel, user trust barriers, and technical impediments harming organic search visibility. 

Using a data-driven approach combining UX heuristics, consumer psychology, and technical crawling tools (Screaming Frog), the audit surfaces three primary conversion friction points and three major technical SEO risks, complete with a prioritized, phased implementation roadmap for Design and Development teams.

---

## 🔍 Key Findings & Impact Estimation

### 1. UX & Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)
* **Above-the-Fold Brand Narrative:** The homepage immediately pushes product grids without establishing brand origin, community values, or UVP, driving up cognitive load for cold traffic.
* **Trust Signals & Social Proof:** Product cards and hero sections lack robust review counts, user-generated content (UGC), or third-party validation, heightening purchase hesitation.
* **Activation & Retention Triggers:** Email capture lacks a concrete first-purchase incentive, and the loyalty/rewards program is buried in the footer, missing key retention and cart-abandonment recovery levers.

### 2. Technical SEO Audit (Screaming Frog Crawl)
* **Crawl Scope:** Analyzed 467 site-wide URLs to evaluate indexation health and structural performance.
* **Server Throttling Risks (429 Errors):** Approximately 74% of HTML pages threw `429 Too Many Requests` status codes during crawl execution, indicating aggressive server or CDN rate-limiting that poses a risk to efficient Googlebot indexing.
* **Metadata & Snippet Gaps:** Critical collection and category paths lacked meta descriptions, forcing search engines to auto-generate snippets and suppressing potential organic Click-Through Rates (CTR).
* **Title Tag Optimization:** Core transactional pages exceeded standard SERP character limits (~60 chars), resulting in truncated keyword visibility in search results.

### 💡 Estimated Business Impact (Projected)
* **Organic Traffic Recovery:** Remediating the server-side rate-limiting (`429` errors) and filling collection-level meta description gaps targets an estimated **10% to 15% recovery** in stable organic CTR and indexation depth over a 60-day window.
* **Funnel Efficiency:** Introducing above-the-fold brand value propositions and inline UGC trust signals targets an estimated **3% to 5% lift** in top-of-funnel conversion rate for mobile traffic.

---

## 🗺️ Implementation Roadmap (Phased Approach)
* **Phase 1 (Immediate / 0–2 weeks):** Resolve CDN rate-limiting bottlenecks, deploy missing meta descriptions, and trim oversized title tags to protect search equity.
* **Phase 2 (Medium-term / 2–6 weeks):** Restructure homepage hero sections for instant brand storytelling, integrate review modules into product cards, and optimize email capture incentives.
* **Phase 3 (Ongoing):** Monitor crawl behavior, indexation metrics, and run continuous A/B testing on core conversion touchpoints.
